## 1. Finding your way around

There are four easy ways to get anywhere:

- **The top menus:**
  - **Compete:** all competitions, open calls, the eligibility check, tracking your application
  - **Showcase:** funded innovators, the community forum
  - **Learn:** how it works, an idea's journey, about the portal, help and support
  - **Partners:** partner spaces, running a competition with CEDA
- **The Explore hub** on the home page: a map of the whole portal. Click a section to open it up and see what's inside, then use the breadcrumb at the top to go back.
- **Search:** press **Ctrl + K** (Windows) or **⌘ + K** (Mac), type what you're looking for, and press Enter.
- **The ⓘ buttons:** on most numbers, badges and charts. Click one for a one-line explanation. Where you see **"For technical judges"**, click it to open the technical detail.

**New here?** Start with:
- the **[Judges' tour](https://ceda-portal--neon-bw-3x0x.us-east4.hosted.app/judges)**: a guided walk through the main screens with one-click sign-in
- **[Follow an idea's journey](https://ceda-portal--neon-bw-3x0x.us-east4.hosted.app/journey)**: one demo idea from start to finish in 16 steps

## 2. Your account

### Create an account
1. Click **Sign in**, then **Create an account** (or go to `/register`).
2. Enter your name, email, phone number, Omang number and date of birth, plus your company name and CIPA number if you have them.
3. **Confirm your email** by clicking the link we send you, and **confirm your phone** with the code we send by SMS.

> **Demo tip:** emails and text messages are shown at **[/demo-inbox](https://ceda-portal--neon-bw-3x0x.us-east4.hosted.app/demo-inbox)**, so you can complete every step without a real inbox or phone. You only see messages sent from your own browser.

### Sign in
Choose whichever suits you:
- **Email and password**
- **One-time code:** go to **Sign in with a code** (`/otp-login`), enter your email or phone, then type the code you receive
- **Demo accounts:** click a role on the login page to sign in instantly

### Forgot or change your password
- **Forgot it:** on the sign-in page click **Forgot password**, enter your email, and follow the link you receive.
- **Change it:** open **Profile** (top-right menu) and choose **Change password**. Staff can also turn on an authenticator app for extra security here.

## 3. Innovators: applying to a competition

**Demo sign-in:** `innovator@demo.ceda.bw`

### Step 1: Check you're eligible
Go to **Compete → Am I eligible?** and answer a few questions. You'll know in about 30 seconds, and if you're not eligible you'll see exactly why.

### Step 2: Find a competition
Go to **Compete → Instances** (all competitions) or **Open calls**. Each competition page shows:
- the **timeline**, with a marker showing where things are now
- **what you need to submit**
- **how you'll be judged**: each scoring area and its weight
- the prizes, the FAQ, and the **rules seal** (proof the rules haven't changed since publishing)

### Step 3: Form your team
If the competition is for teams, create one and invite members by email. Each member adds their role, shareholding and ID details. The portal checks the team rules for you (for example, team size and youth shareholding of more than 50%).

### Step 4: Fill in your application
Click **Apply**. The form saves as you go, so you can come back later. Every question must be answered. Where a question doesn't apply to you, tick **Not applicable**.

### Step 5: Run the submission check
Before you submit, the portal checks your application and files and shows a list:

| Colour | Meaning |
|---|---|
| ✅ Green | All good |
| 🟠 Amber | A warning. You can still submit, but reviewers will see it |
| 🔴 Red | Must be fixed before you can submit (e.g. wrong file type, file too large, proposal over the page limit, or a missing required document) |

Each item tells you how to fix it.

### Step 6: Submit and keep your receipt
Click **Submit**. You get a **signed receipt** with a reference number. Keep it: it proves what you submitted and when. Submissions close exactly at the deadline. The portal can't accept late entries.

### Step 7: Track your application
- **Signed in:** your dashboard shows the stage your application is at.
- **Not signed in:** use **Compete → Track my application** with your reference number and email.

If CEDA asks a question or requests changes, you'll get an alert. Answer in the message thread, update your application and resubmit before the deadline.

### Step 8: Results, feedback and certificates
- Whatever the result, you'll see **feedback for each scoring area**.
- If you win, you get a **certificate** with a QR code that anyone can check, and your idea appears in **Funded innovators**.

### Alerts and help
- **Alerts (free):** open **Subscriptions** in your dashboard to choose email, SMS or portal alerts for a competition or your own application. Every alert has an unsubscribe link.
- **Live chat:** click **Consultant chat** (bottom-right) to talk to a CEDA consultant, 09:00–16:00 on working days. Outside those hours you can leave a message.
- **Your data:** open **My data** in your dashboard to download a copy of everything linked to your account.

## 4. Evaluators: scoring applications

**Demo sign-in:** `evaluator@demo.ceda.bw`

1. **Open your queue** (Evaluator dashboard). It lists the applications assigned to you and when each score is due.
2. **Declare any conflict of interest.** Before an application opens, confirm whether you have any link to it. If you do, it's passed to another evaluator.
3. **Score it.** Scoring is **blind**: you won't see names, ID numbers or company names. Give a score for each criterion. The total is calculated for you. Add feedback for the applicant and, if you like, a private note for CEDA.
4. **Agree a joint score when needed.** If evaluators' scores differ by more than the set amount (e.g. 20 points), the application is flagged. You'll then see the other evaluators' scores, discuss them in a private thread, and record an agreed score and note.

You can reopen a scorecard until the scoring stage closes.

## 5. Adjudication panel: deciding the winners

**Demo sign-in:** `panel@demo.ceda.bw`

1. **Review the ranking.** It's calculated from the stored scores and the published scoring rules, so anyone can re-check it. Where a competition includes a public vote, its share (e.g. 10%) is included.
2. **Shortlist.** Confirm the top applications (e.g. the Top 5), ask applicants for clarification, or decline with a reason.
3. **Decide and award.** Record each decision with a reason. An award creates a certificate and adds the idea to **Funded innovators**.

## 6. CEDA programme admins: running competitions

**Demo sign-in:** `admin@demo.ceda.bw`

### Create a competition (an "Instance")
1. Go to **Instances → New** (Instance Studio).
2. **Simple mode** (the default) takes four steps:
   1. **Basics:** name, description, prizes
   2. **Dates:** each stage of the competition with its start and end
   3. **Who can enter:** eligibility rules and team size
   4. **How it's judged:** scoring areas and weights, which must add up to 100
3. Need more control? Switch to **Advanced** to set the application form and file rules, submission checks, fair-play rules, privacy notice and help channels, and to see the full setup as code.
4. Start quickly with **Start from template** or **Clone** an existing competition.

### Try it before publishing
Click **Simulate** to run sample applications through your rules. You'll see which pass or fail, and why, before anyone applies.

### Publish
Click **Publish**. The rules are **locked with a rules seal**. To change a live competition later you must give a reason. Everyone taking part is notified, and the change is recorded.

### Run it from one page
Open the competition's **Console** to see:
- the current stage and countdown
- registrations and eligibility results
- submission-check problems
- evaluator progress and late scores
- disagreements and agreed scores
- chat activity
- the activity record and version history

### Manage content and reports
- **Content manager:** upload, edit or remove application form templates, applicant guides, FAQs, notices and page content.
- **Reports:** participation, scoring outcomes and time spent in each stage, with CSV export (personal details hidden).

## 7. Partner admins

**Demo sign-ins:** `bdih.admin@demo.ceda.bw`, `btc.admin@demo.ceda.bw` (demo partners)

Partners run their own competitions in their **own branded space** (their logo and colours), using the same Instance Studio as CEDA. They only see their own competitions, users, records and keys. From the partner dashboard you can:
- manage your space's branding and users
- create **API keys** and **webhooks** to connect your own systems
- view your space's activity record

## 8. CEDA consultants: live chat

**Demo sign-in:** `consultant@demo.ceda.bw`

1. Open the **Consultant console** to see waiting chats.
2. **Claim** a chat to answer it. Several consultants can work at once.
3. Chat runs **09:00–16:00 on working days**. Outside those hours, or when no one is online, applicants see a message that no consultant is available and can leave a message instead.
4. **Chat analytics** shows chat volume, response times, resolution rates and the busiest hours.

## 9. Committee observers

**Demo sign-in:** `observer@demo.ceda.bw`

Observers watch a competition **without changing anything**. The observer dashboard updates automatically and shows submissions, screening results, score spreads, agreed scores and the activity record.

## 10. Super admins

**Demo sign-in:** `super@demo.ceda.bw`

- **Users and roles:** add users, change roles, require extra sign-in security for staff.
- **Settings and categories:** platform settings, and the categories used to group competitions and applications (e.g. sector).
- **Activity record:** every important action is listed. Click **Verify** to confirm nothing in the record has been edited or deleted. If something has, the portal shows exactly where.

## 11. Anyone: checking that something is genuine

Go to **[/verify](https://ceda-portal--neon-bw-3x0x.us-east4.hosted.app/verify)** (or scan the QR code on a certificate):
- **Receipts and certificates:** enter the reference and code to confirm they're genuine and unchanged.
- **Competition rules:** on any competition page, click **Verify these rules** to confirm they match what was published.

## 13. Words used on the portal

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Instance** | One competition, with its own dates, entry rules, form and scoring |
| **Template** | A ready-made competition setup to start from |
| **Rules seal** | Proof that a competition's rules haven't changed since it was published |
| **Submission check** | The automatic check of your application and files before you submit |
| **Fair-play report** | The results of the submission check, kept with your receipt |
| **Blind scoring** | Evaluators score without seeing who applied |
| **Agreed score** | The score evaluators agree on together when their scores differ a lot |
| **Activity record** | A tamper-proof list of every important action |
| **Observer** | A committee member who can watch but not change anything |

## 14. If something goes wrong

| Problem | What to do |
|---|---|
| I didn't get my verification email or code | On the demo site, check **/demo-inbox**. Otherwise wait a minute and ask for a new code. After 5 wrong codes, wait 15 minutes. |
| I can't submit | Run the **submission check** and fix any red items. Check the deadline hasn't passed. |
| I can't open an application to score | Declare your conflict of interest first. |
| A page won't load | Check **[Status](https://ceda-portal--neon-bw-3x0x.us-east4.hosted.app/status)**, then report it on **[Support](https://ceda-portal--neon-bw-3x0x.us-east4.hosted.app/support)**. |
| I've forgotten my password | Use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page. |
