# CEDA Innovation Portal: hackathon brief

## The problem

Young Batswana with ideas don't know where to send them, how they'll be judged, or what happened after they applied. CEDA staff receive ideas by email and paper, score them in spreadsheets, and spend time chasing evaluators and answering "any update?" calls. Decisions are hard to explain and hard to report on.

## The solution, in one line

One portal where an idea is submitted once, scored fairly by independent experts, decided by a panel, and tracked in the open from submission to award.

## Who uses it

- **Innovators** check eligibility, apply to a call, save drafts, upload a pitch deck and CIPA certificate, sign and submit, follow five stages, answer CEDA's questions, resubmit, and read anonymous feedback.
- **Evaluators** declare conflicts, score blind on the call's weighted criteria, and write feedback.
- **The adjudication panel** sees a ranked shortlist with every scorecard and disagreement flags, then approves, awards, asks for changes or declines.
- **CEDA programme staff** open calls, set criteria, screen, assign evaluators, chase overdue scores and export reports.

## What makes it different

1. **Fair by design:** blind review, conflict declarations before access, and weighted criteria published on every call.
2. **Nothing gets lost:** five visible stages, notifications at each step, and a message thread per idea.
3. **Proof of what was submitted:** documents are fingerprinted and every submission gets a signed receipt.
4. **Reaches every district:** works on a phone, installs as an app, Setswana on public pages, and a USSD service for founders without data.
5. **Reports CEDA can act on:** participation by district, gender and age; outcome funnel; time to decision; evaluator workload; CSV export.

## Five-minute demo

1. Home page: open calls, the five stages, the eligibility checker.
2. Sign in as **Innovator** (Naledi): one idea awarded, one with changes requested. Open the flagged one: the CEDA note, the message thread and evaluator feedback.
3. Sign in as **Evaluator**: declare no conflict on the pending idea, then score it.
4. Sign in as **Adjudicator**: the ranked shortlist, an idea where evaluators disagree, award it with an amount.
5. Sign in as **Programme admin**: screening queue, assign evaluators, edit a call's criteria, open Reports and export the CSV.
6. Open **USSD demo** and track a reference from a basic phone.
